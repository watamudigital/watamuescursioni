What to know before planning this experience

Mida Creek is a tidal mangrove ecosystem near Watamu. It is a wonderful setting for nature experiences, with routes shaped by tides and the conditions on the day.

Three things to check

  • Tides affect timing and access: confirm the arrangements.
  • Check whether the excursion is on foot, by canoe or by boat.
  • For sunset activities, confirm the return time and meeting point in advance.

Frequently asked questions

Can I explore on foot?

There are paths and access points in the area; ask a local guide about the exact route.

Is sunset always visible?

Cloud cover, season, tides and daily conditions can affect the experience.

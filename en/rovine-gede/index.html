What to know before planning this experience

The archaeological site of Gede tells the story of a Swahili town on Kenya’s coast. Managed by the National Museums of Kenya, the site forms part of UNESCO-recognised heritage.

Three things to check

  • Check current opening hours and prices with the National Museums of Kenya.
  • A qualified guide can add depth to the historical visit.
  • Illustrative forest photos do not necessarily show the Gede site.

Frequently asked questions

Are the ruins a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

Yes. The Historic Town and Archaeological Site of Gedi was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 2024.

Does the visit take a full day?

It depends on where you start and what you combine it with. Ask the organiser for a realistic duration.

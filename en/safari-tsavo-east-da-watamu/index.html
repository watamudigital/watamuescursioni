What to know before planning this experience

Tsavo East is one of the best-known safari destinations accessible from Kenya’s coast. Trip lengths vary: verify travel times, park gate opening hours, accommodation and budget before choosing an itinerary.

Three things to check

  • Request a quote that separates transport, park admission and accommodation.
  • Confirm the vehicle type and cancellation conditions before payment.
  • Wildlife sightings can never be guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Is one day or two days better?

It depends on travel time, budget and your preferred pace. An overnight itinerary can make the transfers easier.

Are park admission fees included?

Not necessarily. Always ask for the full written cost breakdown, including taxes and fees.

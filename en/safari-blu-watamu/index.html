What to know before planning this experience

Watamu’s blue safari is a popular marine outing for visitors staying on the coast. Exact activities depend on season, tides, sea conditions and what the provider offers.

Three things to check

  • Confirm the duration and departure times directly with the organiser.
  • Ask which activities are included and whether marine park entry fees are extra.
  • Check the type of boat, safety equipment and availability for your date.

Frequently asked questions

Is lunch included in a blue safari?

Not necessarily. Meals, drinks and menus depend on the package. Always ask for written confirmation.

Is it suitable for children?

This depends on age, conditions at sea and the vessel. Discuss it with a qualified provider using suitable equipment.

Before booking

Ask for a complete quote

Provide your date, group size and preferences. Check that transport, admissions, equipment, any meals and cancellation conditions are clearly itemised.

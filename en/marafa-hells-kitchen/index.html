What to know before planning this experience

Marafa, also known as Hell’s Kitchen, is a striking eroded landscape inland from the coast, often visited from Watamu or Malindi. Access, routes and timings must be confirmed with local guides.

Three things to check

  • Check transport, access, guide arrangements and expected temperature.
  • Wear suitable shoes and take drinking water.
  • We do not advertise unverified itineraries, opening times or prices.

Frequently asked questions

Is it suitable for everyone?

The terrain and heat can make it demanding. Ask about walking distances, slopes and access conditions.

Can I depart from Watamu?

Watamu is a common starting point for inland excursions; timing and availability depend on the provider.

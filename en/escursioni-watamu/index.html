Practical tips

How to choose an excursion?

Which excursions suit visitors staying in Watamu?

It depends on your interests. Ocean trips and Mida Creek explore the coast; Tsavo East is a land safari, while Gede and Marafa offer culture and unusual landscapes.

How far ahead should I plan?

Check availability and seasonal conditions ahead of time, particularly for overnight safaris and tide-dependent trips.

How do I compare quotes?

Always compare the total price, inclusions, transfers, park entry fees, safety measures and cancellation terms.
